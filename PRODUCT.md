# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Vinext/React Sites scaffold for a private interactive mockup.

## Users

The GMS Executive Team, preparing for and running a fortnightly discussion of internal administrative work.

## Product Purpose

Keep proposed discussion topics, meeting decisions and follow-up work in one shared workspace. Success means the team can see what needs discussion, allocate a lead, record a decision and understand when the next reminder will be sent.

## Positioning

The tracker connects the fortnightly meeting agenda directly to ownership and follow-up, instead of separating meeting notes from action tracking.

## Operating Context

Used before, during and after a fortnightly executive meeting. Microsoft work accounts provide sign-in; Outlook email and calendar are intended to deliver reminders and meeting context.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- This first version is a mockup. It must not authenticate a real Microsoft account, send email or create calendar events.
- The primary workflow is: capture a topic, place it on an upcoming agenda, assign a lead, record a decision and track follow-up.
- Microsoft sign-in, Outlook reminders and calendar linking are represented as clear interactive mock states.
- Illustrative topics, dates and team members must be labelled as sample data.
- The initial interface language is English.

## Brand Commitments

Use the established GMS dashboard design language and authentic supplied GMS 10 Years logo. Preserve the GMS navy, warm-white, action-blue and coral/sky/lavender frosted workspace relationship.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved GMS Frosted Workspace design system and authentic logo assets.
- No confirmed production data, team roster, Microsoft tenant configuration or reminder rules are available; the mockup must not fabricate them as live facts.

## Product Principles

- Make the next discussion and its owner obvious.
- Keep meeting preparation and follow-up in one continuous journey.
- Distinguish sample content and simulated integrations from live organisational data.
- Preserve executive-level scanability while keeping item-level detail close at hand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, readable contrast and responsive layouts are required for the mockup.
